ABOUT ME

It all started with a question: “Who did it?”

I’m Salma Ashour, a Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence student with a growing focus on Data Analysis.

I enjoy taking raw, messy data, finding the patterns hiding inside it, and turning those patterns into insights that people can actually understand and use.

Long before I started learning about data analysis, I was fascinated by crime puzzles and investigation games. You know the kind: someone disappears, a crime happens, and suddenly you’re given a pile of clues, suspicious people, timelines, conversations, locations, and tiny details that may or may not matter.

And naturally, I wanted to figure out what happened.

I’d collect the clues, connect the information, look at where someone had been, who they had talked to, what they had said, and what had changed in the days before the incident.

Then I’d start looking for patterns, inconsistencies, and that one detail that didn’t quite fit.

Eventually, I realized that this way of thinking wasn’t really limited to solving fictional crimes.

It was data analysis.

And what can the data tell us that we haven’t noticed yet?

That curiosity is what pulled me toward Data Analysis.

Today, I use tools like Excel, Power Query, SQL, and Power BI to investigate real-world data, uncover patterns, find meaningful insights, and turn messy information into something people can actually understand and act on.

For me, data analysis isn’t just about making charts look pretty. It’s about asking the right questions, understanding what the numbers are saying, and figuring out why they’re saying it.

So, I guess I never really stopped investigating.

I just replaced the suspects with datasets.
