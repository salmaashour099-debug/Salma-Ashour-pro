Marketing Performance & Customer Behavior Analysis

I analyzed a customer marketing dataset to understand customer value, purchasing behavior, and marketing campaign response.

My responsibilities included data cleaning and transformation, KPI development, customer segmentation, behavioral analysis, and interactive dashboard development using Excel, Power Query, and Power BI.

The analysis covers 2.24K customers, approximately 1M in total spending, and 28.08K purchases, alongside campaign acceptance and response metrics.

Key findings showed that higher-income customers generated higher spending, while customers with higher purchase frequency tended to have both greater spending and higher campaign response rates. Longer-tenure customers also demonstrated higher customer value, with two-year customers averaging 745 in spending and 13.90 purchases, compared with 490 in spending and 10.91 purchases among zero-year customers.

The analysis also identified Wines as the highest-sales product category and Store purchases as the largest purchasing channel at 46.18%. Campaign 4 recorded the highest campaign acceptance count, with 167 acceptances.

HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Analysis

I analyzed an employee HR dataset to understand employee attrition patterns and the characteristics associated with workforce turnover.

My responsibilities included data preparation, KPI development, attrition analysis, employee segmentation, and interactive dashboard development.

The dashboard analyzes 1.464K employees, including 1.229K active employees and 239 employees who left, resulting in an overall 16.325% attrition rate.

The analysis found that Administration accounted for the largest share of attrition at 36.82%, followed by Sales at 22.59%. Attrition was also examined across salary levels, age groups, experience, distance from work, gender, job roles, and job satisfaction to identify areas requiring further HR investigation.

Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

I analyzed the Superstore dataset to understand sales performance, profitability, regional performance, customer segments, and product-level results.

My responsibilities included data preparation, KPI analysis, regional and segment analysis, product profitability analysis, trend analysis, and dashboard development.

The dashboard analyzes approximately $2.30M in sales and $286.4K in profit. The West region generated the highest sales at approximately $725K, while the Consumer segment represented 50% of total sales. Technology was the highest-sales category, generating approximately $836K.

The analysis also highlighted differences in product profitability, with Chairs generating approximately $27K in profit, while Tables recorded a negative profit. Monthly analysis showed stronger sales toward the end of the displayed year, with November reaching approximately $76K.

Uber Booking & Revenue Analysis

I analyzed an Uber booking dataset to understand booking performance, revenue generation, vehicle-type performance, and operational trends.

My responsibilities included data preparation, KPI analysis, booking-status analysis, monthly trend analysis, vehicle-level revenue analysis, and interactive dashboard development.

The dashboard covers 93K completed bookings, 57K lost bookings, 52M in revenue, and 2.51M in total trip distance, with additional analysis of vehicle types, locations, and service ratings.

The analysis showed that Auto generated the highest vehicle-level revenue at approximately 12.9M, followed by Bike at 11.5M and Go Mini at 10.3M. Monthly completed bookings fluctuated throughout the year while monthly revenue remained comparatively stable, generally ranging from 4.0M to 4.6M.

The dashboard also tracks booking outcomes, pickup/drop locations, average trip distance, and customer and driver ratings, providing a consolidated view of operational and revenue performance.
